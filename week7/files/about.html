About

I'm Najaf Shehzad, an ML engineer in training, focused on turning real-world problems into working systems, not just trained models in a notebook.

My final year project, TrafficSmart, started from a problem my university flagged as genuinely government-relevant: city roads too congested for both planners and commuters to navigate well. Rather than working with a clean, ready-made dataset, I collected and annotated over 7,000 real traffic images myself, and built the detection pipeline end-to-end, from raw footage to a working monitoring dashboard.

I'm building toward applied ML engineering roles where the work has to actually run in the real world, not just perform well in a notebook. That means I care as much about honest evaluation and real deployment constraints as I do about model accuracy.
